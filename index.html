Learn it by playing it

A lab for people who don't accept "it just works":

  1. Play it by hand. Before any algorithm runs, you do its job yourself — move the block, probe the slot, follow the pointer.
  2. Predict, then watch. Lock in a guess, then the world runs and grades you against your own run.
  3. Ask "why this design?" Every world ends with the trade-offs: why this structure and not another.
  4. Collect ideas. Terms land in your notebook only after you've seen the thing happen.
